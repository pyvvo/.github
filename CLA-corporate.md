# pyvvo Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0

This agreement lets a company or another legal entity authorize its employees to contribute to the projects of pyvvo.
The entity keeps the copyright in the contributions. The agreement gives pyvvo the rights it needs to distribute them,
including under license terms other than the ones a project uses today. An employee who contributes on the entity's
behalf still accepts the individual agreement when the CLA assistant asks, unless pyvvo has added the employee to the
CLA assistant's allow list after this agreement was signed.

## 1. Definitions

- **"You"** means the legal entity that signs this agreement, together with every entity that controls it, that it
  controls, or that is under common control with it. **"Control"** means the direct or indirect ownership of more than
  fifty percent of the voting securities or other ownership interests of an entity, or the power to direct its
  management, by contract or otherwise.
- **"pyvvo"** means pyvvo, the owner of the pyvvo organization on GitHub (https://github.com/pyvvo).
- **"Project"** means any software project that pyvvo owns or manages, including every repository of the pyvvo
  organization on GitHub.
- **"Designated Employee"** means an individual named in Schedule A, as You update it under section 8.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including a change or an addition to an existing work, that a
  Designated Employee Submits to pyvvo on Your behalf for inclusion in a Project.
- **"Submit"** means to send any electronic or written communication to pyvvo or to its representatives, such as a pull
  request, a patch, an issue or a comment in a system that pyvvo manages, in order to discuss or improve a Project. A
  communication that is marked in writing as "Not a Contribution" is not Submitted.

## 2. Copyright license

You grant pyvvo, and every recipient of software that pyvvo distributes, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
no-charge, royalty-free and irrevocable license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly
perform, sublicense and distribute Your Contributions and such derivative works. pyvvo may license Your Contributions
under any terms, including terms other than the license under which a Project is distributed when they are Submitted.

## 3. Patent license

You grant pyvvo, and every recipient of software that pyvvo distributes, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
no-charge and royalty-free patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer
the Project to which a Contribution was Submitted. The license covers only the patent claims that You can license and
that Your Contribution infringes, alone or combined with that Project.

This patent license is irrevocable, with one exception. If any entity starts patent litigation, including a
cross-claim or a counterclaim, alleging that Your Contribution, or the Project to which it was Submitted, infringes a
patent, then the patent licenses that this agreement grants to that entity for that Contribution or Project end on the
date when the litigation is filed.

## 4. Your representations

You represent that:

1. You are legally entitled to grant the licenses above, and the person who signs this agreement is authorized to sign
   it on Your behalf.
2. Each Designated Employee is authorized to Submit Contributions on Your behalf.
3. Each of Your Contributions is an original creation of Your Designated Employees, except work of others that is
   Submitted as section 6 describes.
4. Each of Your Contributions includes the complete details of every third-party license or other restriction that You
   know of and that applies to any part of it, such as a related patent or trademark.

## 5. No support and no warranty

You are not expected to provide support for Your Contributions, unless You choose to. Unless applicable law requires it
or You agree to it in writing, You provide Your Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind,
express or implied, including any warranty or condition of title, non-infringement, merchantability or fitness for a
particular purpose.

## 6. Work of others

If You want to Submit work that is not an original creation of Your Designated Employees, You Submit it separately from
any Contribution. You identify its source and the complete details of every license or other restriction that You know
of, such as a related patent, trademark or license agreement, and You mark it clearly as "Submitted on behalf of a third
party: [name]".

## 7. Changes in circumstances

You agree to tell pyvvo about any fact or circumstance that You become aware of and that would make Your
representations in this agreement inaccurate.

## 8. Designated Employees

Schedule A lists the individuals who may Submit Contributions on Your behalf, with their names and GitHub usernames.
You update Schedule A by sending pyvvo a new list. You tell pyvvo when an individual must leave the list.

## 9. Your rights

Except for the licenses that this agreement grants, You keep all right, title and interest in Your Contributions.
